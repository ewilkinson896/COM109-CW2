Who We Are

Our Café

A welcoming space for students, professionals and visitors.

York Street Coffee is an independent coffee shop located in Belfast, close to Ulster University. We are passionate about serving high-quality coffee, fresh bakery goods and providing a comfortable environment for studying, meeting friends or enjoying a quiet break.

Our Mission

Our mission is simple: to serve excellent coffee and create a friendly atmosphere where everyone feels welcome. We focus on quality ingredients, great customer service and supporting our local community.
