Our Story

York Street Coffee is an independent coffee shop located in Belfast, serving premium coffee, freshly baked pastries and delicious treats.


Located beside Ulster University Belfast, we are a popular destination for students, staff and visitors looking for a relaxing environment to study, meet friends or enjoy a coffee.


We are committed to providing excellent customer service and high-quality products in a warm and welcoming atmosphere.
