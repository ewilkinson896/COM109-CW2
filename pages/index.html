Our Story

York Street Coffee is an independent coffee shop located in the heart of Belfast, serving premium coffee, freshly baked pastries and a range of delicious homemade treats. Our goal is to create a warm and welcoming environment where customers can relax and enjoy high-quality food and drink.

Conveniently situated close to Ulster University's Belfast campus, York Street Coffee is a popular destination for students, university staff and visitors. Whether you're looking for a quiet place to study, meet friends or take a break between lectures, our café offers the perfect setting.

From expertly crafted espresso-based drinks to freshly prepared bakery products, we take pride in delivering excellent customer service and a memorable café experience. Whether you are stopping by for your morning coffee or enjoying an afternoon treat, we look forward to welcoming you.
